## Information used for accounts and services

The portal processes information you or authorized clinic staff provide to identify you, manage access and deliver the requested service. Depending on the feature, this includes:

- Your username, email address, phone number, clinic and patient identifiers, account status and contact changes.
- Information used to match an invitation, such as your email, date of birth and health card details. Verification records and encrypted data needed to deliver invitations or other messages may be retained.
- Protected password-verification values, sessions, verification challenges, recovery requests and their outcomes. Your password is stored as a verification hash rather than a readable password.
- Information about linked devices, including public keys, identifiers, a device label and approval or removal status, along with document-delivery and receipt records.
- Information needed for any other enabled clinic service. **[DESCRIBE THE ACTUAL SERVICE AND THE INFORMATION IT USES.]**

These records support identity checks, delivery, recovery, security and the operation of your clinic's service. **[CLINIC AND LEGAL REVIEW TO CONFIRM PURPOSES, APPLICABLE AUTHORITY OR CONSENT ARRANGEMENTS, AND ANY OTHER USES OR DISCLOSURES.]** Providing information for an account does not by itself authorize every possible use of your health information.

## Signing in and messages

Browser sessions and security cookies help keep you signed in and protect account actions. The browser also stores a language preference. Session and verification records help the portal recognize authorized access, expire access and investigate failures. Use a private device where possible and sign out on a shared device.

The service can send verification codes, account notices and reset links through its configured email or SMS services. Those providers receive your address or number and the message they must deliver. Email and SMS can expose information through mailboxes, shared devices, notification previews and provider records. A reset link or code can grant access, so do not share it.

The portal can hold encrypted copies of passwords used to open clinic email attachments and reveal them after authorized access. This differs from the encryption used for myCarlos delivery packages. **[IDENTIFY THE EMAIL AND SMS PROVIDERS, COUNTRIES OF PROCESSING, MESSAGE CONTENT, AND HOW LONG THEY KEEP DELIVERY RECORDS.]**

## Encrypted delivery to myCarlos

Where enabled, the clinic sends a separate package encrypted for each approved device. The portal holds the sealed package for pickup and does not hold the device's private key needed to open it. The receiving app checks the package and sends a receipt after filing it.

This encryption does not hide the entire relationship from the portal. The portal handles your account and routing information, device identifiers, package sizes, times, delivery status, cryptographic fingerprints and receipts. Some receipt information supports checking delivery and the receiving vault's history. Clinical titles and document contents are inside the sealed package; account, security and routing records are separate.

Accepted receipts or removal of a linked device cause waiting package contents to be removed from the active mailbox. Waiting packages become unavailable for pickup no later than seven days after creation. Removing expired package contents from active storage depends on the service's cleanup operation. Delivery metadata, receipts, security records and backups have separate retention arrangements. **[OPERATOR TO CONFIRM THAT MAILBOX EXPIRY AND CLEANUP ARE ENABLED AND OPERATED, AND COMPLETE THE RETENTION DETAILS BELOW.]** Removing a device or package does not delete the clinic's record or a copy already received by myCarlos.

If document notifications are offered, an external delivery service may receive a device destination and a generic notice. **[ONLY INCLUDE AN ENABLED NOTIFICATION SERVICE AFTER CONFIRMING ITS PROVIDER, DESTINATION DATA, MESSAGE CONTENT AND LOCATION.]** A notification is separate from delivery of the encrypted document.

## Connection information and security records

The service and its hosting infrastructure process connection information such as internet addresses, request times, routes, response status and security outcomes. Some application records use protected references to addresses. Proxy, hosting and provider records may contain additional connection information.

The portal keeps an audit history of account and security actions, access to protected information, delivery and relevant staff or system actions. These records can identify an account, clinic, staff member or linked device and show when actions occurred. They are used to operate the service and investigate incidents. Encryption of delivery packages does not make these records anonymous.

## How long information is kept

Different records have different lifetimes. Ending a session, expiring a code or removing a waiting package does not delete an account, security history, delivery receipt or clinic record. Some expired operational records require scheduled cleanup. Backups and retained audit copies can outlast removal from the active service.
